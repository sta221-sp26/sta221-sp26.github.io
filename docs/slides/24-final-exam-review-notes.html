Let \(\pi_i\) be the probability an individual is diagnosed with lung cancer. We fit the model

\[ \log\Big(\frac{\hat{\pi}_i}{1-\hat{\pi}_i}\Big) = \hat{\beta}_0 + \hat{\beta}_1 \text{birdkeepingYes} \]
